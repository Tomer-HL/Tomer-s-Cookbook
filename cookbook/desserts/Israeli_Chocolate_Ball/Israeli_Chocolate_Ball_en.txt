Israeli Chocolate Balls

Position: center center
Zoom: 100%

Description
Chocolate balls are as iconic as falafel in Israel—at least for kids. Known as *kadorei shokolad* (כדורי שוקולד) in Hebrew, these simple treats are a quintessential part of Israeli childhood. They’re incredibly easy to make and are a perfect recipe to prepare together with the kids.

Ingredients
8.5 oz (240 g) tea biscuits / Petit Beurre biscuits
4 oz (115 g) unsalted butter or margarine, melted
8 tbsp sugar
5 tbsp cocoa powder
¼ cup (60 ml) milk or soy milk (for a pareve version)
1 tbsp vanilla extract
**Optional for coating:** 3.5 oz (100 g) dark chocolate, melted/Coconut flakes/sprinkles, or crushed biscuits.

Instructions
1. **Crush the biscuits:**
Place the biscuits in a bag and crush them with a rolling pin. Leave some larger pieces to give the chocolate balls a nice crunchy texture.
2. **Make the mixture:**
Add the sugar, cocoa powder, vanilla extract, and melted butter or margarine. Mix well, preferably by hand. If desired, add the melted dark chocolate and mix until combined.
3. **Add the milk:** Gradually add the milk until the mixture becomes slightly sticky and holds together. Be careful not to add too much.
4. **Shape the balls:** Take small portions of the mixture and roll them between your hands to form small balls.
5. **Coat:**
Roll each ball in coconut flakes, sprinkles, or crushed biscuits.
6. **Chill:**
Refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving. Keep refrigerated.

**Yield:** Approximately 25–30 chocolate balls, depending on size.
