Biscuit Cake

Position: center center
Zoom: 100%

Description
The most nostalgic and beloved dessert in Israel. It’s easy to make, requires no baking, and is perfect for making with kids. The combination of rich vanilla cream and soft biscuit layers creates a perfect result with minimal effort.

Ingredients
1 pack (250g) Petit Beurre biscuits (Vanilla or Chocolate)
1 cup milk (for dipping the biscuits)
Chocolate shavings (50g dark chocolate) for topping
For the Cream:
1 cup (250ml) heavy whipping cream, chilled
½ cup milk
1 pack (80g) instant vanilla pudding mix
250g (approx. 1 cup) natural yogurt (4%–5%) or soft white cheese/sour cream (5%–9% fat)

Instructions
1. Prepare the Cream:
In a large bowl, combine the heavy cream, ½ cup of milk, and the instant pudding mix. Whip until the mixture is stable and airy.
Gently fold in the yogurt (or soft cheese) using a spatula until the cream is smooth and uniform.
2. Dip the Biscuits:
Pour 1 cup of milk into a shallow bowl. Dip each biscuit into the milk for only 1–2 seconds.
Tip: Do not soak them, or they will become mushy!
3. Assemble the Cake:
Base: Arrange a single layer of dipped biscuits at the bottom of a small rectangular or square baking dish.
Layering: Spread a layer of the cream evenly over the biscuits.
Repeat: Add another layer of dipped biscuits, then cream. Repeat until you have at least 3 layers of biscuits.
Top: Finish with a final layer of cream and smooth it out with a spatula.
4. Garnish and Chill:
Sprinkle the grated dark chocolate over the top.
Refrigerate for at least 2 hours to set. For the best texture, refrigerate for 4 hours or overnight.
5. Serving:
Cut and serve directly from the dish.
Pro Tip: For a semi-frozen treat, chill in the fridge for 4 hours first, then move to the freezer for a short time before serving.
💡 Key Tips:
Stable Cream: Ensure your cream is whipped until stiff peaks form to prevent the cake from "sliding."
Quick Dip: A very quick dip in the milk is all you need; the biscuits will continue to soften from the cream's moisture.
Texture Balance: Using yogurt provides a lighter, tangier taste, while soft cheese gives it a richer, more traditional Israeli texture.
