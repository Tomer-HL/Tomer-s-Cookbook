Yemenite Beef Soup

Position: center center
Zoom: 100%

Description
Yemenite beef soup is traditionally served as the main course on holiday evenings and at social gatherings. The flavorful broth is seasoned with hawaij, a traditional Yemenite spice blend that can be found in many Jewish markets. Hawaij is also known for its high turmeric content, making this hearty soup both flavorful and nutritious.

Ingredients
2 tbsp olive oil
2–3 medium onions, peeled and diced
2 lbs beef stew meat with bones
1 tbsp paprika
½ tsp cumin
2 tbsp hawaij spice blend
8–10 cloves garlic
2–3 carrots, peeled and cut into 2-inch pieces
3–4 medium red or Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled and cut into quarters
2 celery stalks, cut into 2-inch pieces
1 bunch cilantro, cleaned, rinsed, and chopped as desired, plus more for garnish
3–4 whole allspice berries
2 bay leaves
Salt, to taste

Instructions
1. Place the beef pieces in an 8-quart stockpot.
2. Add about 12–14 cups of water to the pot, or enough to fill it about two-thirds of the way.
3. Bring the water to a boil and simmer for a few minutes, skimming off any foam that rises to the surface.
4. Add the hawaij, cumin, paprika, salt, onion, and garlic cloves to the pot and stir to combine. 
5. Reduce the heat to medium and let the soup cook for 1½ hours, until the meat begins to soften.
6. Check the soup periodically to make sure it remains at a gentle simmer, with small bubbles rather than a rapid boil. Add more water if needed.
7. Add the potato chunks, carrots, celery, and cilantro. 
8. Bring the soup back to a low simmer and continue cooking for another 30 minutes, or until the carrots and potatoes are tender.
9. Garnish with additional fresh cilantro before serving.
