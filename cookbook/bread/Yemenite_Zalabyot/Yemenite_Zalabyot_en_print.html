Yemenite Zalabyot

Zalabia is a type of fried dough, similar to a doughnut. While the sweet version makes for a delicious dessert, zalabia is traditionally enjoyed as a savory dish, served with crushed fresh tomatoes and hard-boiled eggs.

Ingredients

  • 7 cups (1 kg / 2.2 lbs) all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp dry yeast
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 4–5 cups water
  • Vegetable oil or canola oil, for frying
  • For Serving
  • Hard-boiled eggs
  • Serves 25–30 zalabyot.

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, combine the flour, yeast, sugar, salt, and water. The dough should be very soft and wet.
  2. Knead the dough for about 10 minutes, until the ingredients are well combined. Cover and let it rest for about 10 minutes.
  3. After the first rest, knead the dough again for a few more minutes. Cover and let it rise for about 1 hour. The dough will be very sticky, so coat your hands with a little oil or water when handling it.
  4. Heat the vegetable or canola oil in a deep frying pan over medium heat. The oil should reach about two-thirds of the way up the sides of the pan.
  5. Take a ball of dough about the size of your fist and gently flatten it to approximately 1 inch thick. Carefully place it into the hot oil. The oil will bubble around the dough.
  6. Fry until golden brown on one side, then carefully flip and fry the other side until golden brown as well. Make sure the dough is not too thick and the oil is not too hot; otherwise, the inside may remain doughy.
  7. Savory Serving Suggestion
    Zalabia is best served hot with crushed fresh tomatoes mixed with finely chopped cilantro, garlic, salt, and pepper to taste. For a spicy kick, add Yemenite schug. Traditionally, it is also served with sliced hard-boiled eggs.
  8. Sweet Serving Suggestion
    While still warm, coat the zalabia on all sides with granulated sugar.
